Executive Summary

Aldergate Heating & Air loses jobs every week to the first competitor who responds. Fieldwork Studio proposes an AI lead engagement system that texts every new website lead within 30 seconds, qualifies the service need over SMS, and books the appointment directly into Google Calendar — with no staff intervention required.

The system replaces hours of manual texting with an instant, always-on conversation, and gives Aldergate a dashboard to monitor every lead, conversation, and booking as it happens.

Background

Aldergate receives leads through its website, but response time is inconsistent. Leads submitted outside business hours, during a job, or during a busy stretch often wait minutes to hours for a reply — and by then, many homeowners have already contacted another contractor.

The underlying issues are common across the trade: slow response times, missed after-hours opportunities, staff spending significant time on manual texting, low conversion from inquiry to booked appointment, and inconsistent communication from lead to lead. An automated system that engages every lead immediately closes this gap.

Proposed Solution

We will build and deploy an AI SMS assistant around the following flow:

  • Instant acknowledgment. The moment a website lead comes in, the AI texts the customer by name within 30 seconds, introducing itself and acknowledging the request.
  • Qualification. The AI asks natural follow-up questions — issue type, urgency, when it started, location — and answers common questions on service areas, availability, and basic pricing.
  • Booking. The AI checks live Google Calendar availability, offers open time slots, confirms the customer's choice, and creates the calendar event automatically, preventing double-booking.
  • Confirmation. The customer receives an SMS confirmation, and the appointment appears on Aldergate's calendar with no manual entry.
  • Human handoff. Staff can take over any conversation at any time, and the AI escalates automatically for emergencies or low-confidence situations per Aldergate's business rules.
