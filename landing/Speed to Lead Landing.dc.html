Frequently asked
Does this replace my office staff?+

No — it handles the first response and booking so your staff isn't chasing texts. Staff can take over any conversation at any time.

What if it can't answer a question?+

It escalates to a human when its confidence is low or when the customer asks for a person — nothing gets stuck.

Does it work outside business hours?+

Yes — after-hours is where most leads are lost. It replies and books around the clock, respecting the hours you configure.

What does it connect to?+

Your website's lead form, an SMS provider (like Twilio), and Google Calendar for real-time availability and bookings.

Is customer data secure?+

Yes — data is encrypted in transit, storage is access-controlled by role, and key actions are logged for audit.
